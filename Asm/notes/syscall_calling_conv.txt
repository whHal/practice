Syscall Number: rax

    Tells the kernel what are you doing.
    
    syscall nums:
        - 0   = read
        - 1   = write
        - 2   = open
        - 3   = close
        - 4   = stat
        - 8   = lseek
        - 9   = mmap
        - 12  = brk
        - 39  = getpid
        - 60  = exit
        - 79  = getcwd
        - 318 = getrandom 

Syscall Argument Register Order:
    
    1. rdi
    2. rsi
    3. rdx
    4. r10
    5. r8
    6. r9
    7. rax (return value)
